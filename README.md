# E-Commerce Logistics Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end Business Analytics project that transforms raw logistics data into actionable business insights using Excel, MySQL, SQL, Power BI, and DAX.

---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce logistics dataset containing 10,999 customer orders to evaluate delivery performance, warehouse efficiency, shipment utilization, and customer satisfaction.

The objective is to help logistics managers make data-driven decisions through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## Problem Statement

E-commerce companies generate thousands of logistics records every day. However, raw operational data alone cannot identify:

- Why deliveries are delayed
- Which shipment mode is most utilized
- How warehouses perform comparatively
- Whether delivery performance affects customer ratings

This project converts raw logistics data into meaningful KPIs and executive-level business insights.

---

## Business Insights

### 1. Delivery Performance
Only around **43%** of deliveries are completed on time, indicating significant operational improvement opportunities.

### 2. Shipment Utilization
Ship transportation handles the highest volume of customer orders and remains the primary logistics channel.

### 3. Warehouse Performance
Warehouse blocks show varying operational efficiency, enabling performance benchmarking.

### 4. Customer Satisfaction
Customers receiving on-time deliveries generally provide better ratings, demonstrating the relationship between service quality and customer experience.

---

## Executive Recommendation

- Reduce delayed deliveries by improving route planning.
- Optimize ship capacity during peak demand.
- Replicate best-performing warehouse practices.
- Improve delivery reliability to increase customer satisfaction.
